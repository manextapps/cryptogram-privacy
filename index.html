1. Who we are

Cryptogram: Quote Puzzles (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published on Google Play by the developer MaNext. This policy explains how the Android app handles information. For questions, see Contact.

2. About the app

Cryptogram: Quote Puzzles is a word puzzle game: you decode famous public domain quotes in which every letter was swapped for another letter. It includes 400 puzzles in three levels, a daily puzzle, hints, statistics and settings. Puzzles are played offline. An internet connection is used only for ads, for the ad consent form, for the optional purchase through Google Play, and when you choose to open a link such as this policy.

3. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not operate a server for this app and do not collect, receive or store personal data about you. The app has no account or sign in, no analytics or crash reporting tools of our own, and does not use Firebase.

Ads in the app are provided by Google, which collects some data directly through its software development kit (SDK). That is explained in section 5.

If you email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

4. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following information locally on your device so the game works. It is not sent to us.

  • Game progress: solved puzzles and the letters you have entered in puzzles you have started.
  • Daily puzzle results and your daily streak.
  • Statistics: puzzles solved, hints used and solve times.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, highlighting, large letters, theme, and whether you have seen the how to play card.
  • Counters that pace full screen ads (how many puzzles you have solved since the last one and when it was shown).
  • Whether you have bought the option to remove ads, as confirmed by Google Play.

Google's ad, consent and billing libraries also store some information on your device, such as your consent choices, so that they work correctly.

5. Advertising and Google's ad SDK

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request ad consent where required. The app shows:

  • Banner ads at the bottom of the menu and the puzzle list, never on the puzzle board while you solve.
  • Interstitial (full screen) ads, only between puzzles after you tap Next, and never during a puzzle.
  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one for an extra hint after the free hints. If no ad is available, you get the hint anyway.

Personalized ads are based on information such as your interests and past activity. Ads that are not personalized are based on more limited context, such as the app itself and your general (not precise) location. Depending on your choices, your region and your device settings, Google may show you personalized, not personalized or limited ads. Learn more in How Google uses cookies and similar technologies in advertising.

6. In app purchase

The app offers one optional one time purchase, Remove ads, which removes every ad and makes all hints free. The purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles your payment details under the Google Payments Privacy Notice; we never receive them. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase so that it can turn ads off, including after you reinstall (Settings → Restore purchase).

7. Consent and your ad choices

Where it is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and, where we have enabled it, certain U.S. states), the app shows Google's consent message when it starts. You can accept, decline or manage your choices.

When privacy options are available for your region, you can review or change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. If your choices mean ads cannot be requested, the game still works and extra hints are given without an ad.

You can also manage ad personalization for your Google Account at My Ad Center.

8. Your advertising ID

Android provides a resettable advertising ID. On most devices you can reset or delete it in Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by device and Android version). Deleting it stops apps from accessing it for advertising; resetting it replaces it with a new random ID. Some devices also offer ad privacy settings for the Android Privacy Sandbox in the same area.

9. Android permissions

The app does not ask you for permission to use location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, storage or notifications. The installed app includes the following permissions, none of which shows a permission prompt:

  • Internet access and network state: to load ads and the consent form, and to talk to Google Play for the optional purchase.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): for the optional Remove ads purchase.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the ad SDK read the advertising ID.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): let the ad SDK use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features where the device supports them.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: required by the Android WorkManager library that the ad SDK uses for background tasks. The app itself does not run a foreground service.

10. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself does not collect personal information from anyone. Ads in the app are served by Google, which collects the data described in section 5. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions or concerns, please contact us.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Our servers: we have none, so we keep no copy of your game data.
  • On your device: game data stays until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Google: data collected by Google's ad SDK and Google Play is retained according to Google's policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google retains data.

12. Device backup

The app allows Android's backup feature to include your game progress and settings, so they can be restored if you set up a new device. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may store this data in your Google Account backup or move it when you move to a new device, under Google's terms and your device settings. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

13. Sharing and links

We do not sell or share your personal data, because we do not receive any. Data sent through the ad SDK and Google Play is described above. Apart from that, the app sends information only when you choose to:

  • Open this privacy policy: it opens in your browser. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Contact us: the app opens your email app with our address. You decide whether to send anything.

14. Security

Because we do not collect your data, there is no copy of it on our side to protect or lose. Game data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Google states that the data its ad SDK collects is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and U.S. state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal data. These can include the right to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • We do not hold personal data about you, so in most cases there is nothing for us to access or delete. Your game data is on your device and you can delete it as described in section 11.
  • For data collected by Google through the ad SDK, use your ad choices in the app (section 7), your device's ad settings (section 8), and Google's tools described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can always contact us, and we will help as far as we are able.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app or the services it uses change. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also take reasonable steps to let you know, for example through the app's release notes.
